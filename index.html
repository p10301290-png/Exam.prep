Beginner-first order: Topics are arranged by phase, then subject, then prerequisite-friendly order. In Maths, start with number basics → BODMAS → fractions/decimals → HCF/LCM → square roots → Percentage. Percentage comes before Ratio, Profit/Loss and Interest.
